About us

A small team
that means it.

Mother Touch is a woman-owned Cape Town cleaning business. Still small, still personal, still run by the person who answers the phone.

“Clean the way you’d want your own home cleaned.”

We started with one house, one client, and a reputation to earn. Years on, it’s the same approach that’s grown us.

Started small, stayed small.

Mother Touch began the way most small businesses in Cape Town do. One person, one van, and the word of a client who kept recommending us.

We’ve added people since, and more services, but the shape of the business has stayed the same. The person you reach is the person who runs it, not a call centre. The team on the clean is the team we’d send to our own homes.

Why “Mother Touch”.

The name is a promise. The kind of clean a careful mother does when she’s hosting. Not showy, not rushed, just thorough and warm.

That means we work slower than cheap services and faster than fussy ones. We care that the house feels good when we leave, not just that boxes got ticked.

Who we work with.

Private households, mostly. Small offices around Woodstock, Observatory and the City Bowl. A growing list of Airbnb hosts across the Atlantic Seaboard and Southern Suburbs.

If your space fits one of our services, we probably clean something similar every week. If it doesn’t, ask anyway.

What we hold to

Four small promises.

i.

On time, every time

We arrive when we said we would. If something changes, you hear from us before the slot, not after.

ii.

Respectful of your home

Shoes off where asked, pets looked after, valuables untouched. You should feel comfortable with us in the space.

iii.

Honest about what we can do

If a stain won’t come out, we’ll tell you. If a job needs more time than quoted, we’ll ask before adding it.

iv.

Clean until it’s right

If something’s missed, we come back. No arguments, no deductions. We’d rather do it twice than argue once.
