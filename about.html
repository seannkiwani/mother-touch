A note from our founder

Why I started Mother Touch.

I started Mother Touch with a simple idea: inviting someone into your home should feel comfortable. You should be able to explain what matters to you and know your space will be treated with care.

For me, a good clean is in the everyday details. A kitchen ready for supper. A bathroom that feels fresh. A welcoming room for the next guest. That is the care I want Mother Touch to bring to every visit.

We start by listening, agree on the work, and keep you informed. Whether you need a regular home clean or help preparing a property, we want the experience to feel straightforward and personal.

What you can expect.

Before a visit, we talk through your rooms, priorities and any special instructions. We agree on the cleaning tasks, access arrangements and products or equipment needed so you know what to expect.

During the clean, the focus is on the work we have agreed. If something needs more time or a different approach, we discuss it rather than adding work without your approval.

Afterwards, we welcome your feedback. If something needs attention, get in touch so we can understand the concern and agree on the next step.
