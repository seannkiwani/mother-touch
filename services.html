Home & domestic cleaning

Cleaning tasks we can agree on

  • Kitchen worktops, hob surfaces and sink.
  • Bathroom basins, toilets, showers and mirrors.
  • Dusting accessible surfaces and vacuuming or mopping floors.
  • General tidying and emptying household bins.

Deep cleaning

Extra attention for a space that needs more than its usual routine. We plan the clean around the condition of the property.

Cleaning tasks we can agree on

  • Detailed cleaning of agreed kitchen and bathroom areas.

  • Interior appliance or cupboard cleaning when specifically agreed.

Office cleaning

Cleaning for offices and shared workspaces, with a schedule that suits your premises.

Cleaning tasks we can agree on

04

Airbnb & holiday rentals

Changeover cleaning planned around guest departures and arrivals, so your next stay begins with a welcoming space.

Get a free quote →

Cleaning tasks we can agree on

  • Cleaning of bedrooms, bathrooms and living areas.

Move-in & move-out cleaning

Get a free quote →

Cleaning tasks we can agree on
