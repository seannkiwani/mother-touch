Five services, laid out plainly. What’s included, roughly how long it takes, and the kind of property it’s for.

01

Residential cleaning

The weekly clean for the home you actually live in. Bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, living areas, done properly without rushing.

Book a home clean

What’s included

  • Full kitchen clean: counters, hob, splashback, exterior of appliances, sink.
  • Bathrooms top to bottom: basins, taps, toilets, showers, mirrors, floor.
  • Dust, wipe and sanitise all accessible surfaces.
  • Mop or vacuum every floor.
  • Beds made, laundry folded on request.
  • Rubbish taken out and bags replaced.
Good forFlats & houses
How long3–5 hours
How oftenOnce off, or weekly

Office cleaning

Workspace cleaning that keeps a team productive. Done before, after or around your hours, depending on your office.

Book an office clean

What’s included

  • Desks, chairs and shared surfaces wiped and sanitised.
  • Bathrooms kept stocked, cleaned, dried.
  • Kitchenette: counters, sink, kettle, microwave, dishwasher load if left.
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped.
  • Rubbish and recycling emptied and replaced.
  • Meeting rooms reset between uses.
Good forSmall-to-mid offices
How long2–4 hours
How oftenDaily or weekly
03

Airbnb & holiday homes

Turnover cleans for short-stay rentals. Linens, bathrooms, kitchen, trash, all reset for your next guest. On request, we send a photo of the finished unit.

Set up turnovers

What’s included

  • Full strip and remake of beds. Linen turnover included.
  • Bathroom reset: fresh towels, toiletries topped up.
  • Kitchen reset: dishwasher ran, surfaces clean, supplies checked.
  • All floors vacuumed and mopped.
  • Trash out, bins fresh, exterior spot-check.
  • Optional photo report sent to you after the clean.
Good forAirbnb & short-stays
How long2–3 hours per unit
How oftenPer booking
04

Deep cleaning

The clean a space needs after a long gap, before a move, or just to get everything back to a baseline. Slower, more thorough, into the places a regular clean skips.

Book a deep clean

What’s included

  • Inside oven, inside fridge, inside microwave.
  • Behind furniture, under beds, inside wardrobes if emptied.
  • Skirtings, door frames and light switches wiped.
  • Window frames and sills, interior-side.
  • Limescale descaled on taps and shower doors.
  • Grout spot-cleaned where it will take.
Good forMove-in / move-out
How long6–10 hours
How oftenOnce a quarter, or once off
