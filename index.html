Questions

A few useful
answers.

Which cleaning services do you offer?

Home and domestic cleaning, deep cleaning, office cleaning, Airbnb and holiday rental cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, and ongoing maid services.

Can I arrange regular cleaning?

Yes. Ask about weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits in your area. We’ll confirm availability when you enquire.

How do I request a quote?

Share your suburb, property size, the service you need and a preferred date, by form or WhatsApp. Quotations are free and tailored to your space.

What areas of Cape Town do you cover?

Cape Town and surrounding suburbs. Share your suburb when you enquire and we’ll confirm whether we can reach you.

Is an enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. The clean is confirmed with Mother Touch once the service, scope and date are agreed.

Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?

Tell us whether you need products and equipment supplied, or plan to provide your own. We’ll confirm what is included when discussing your quote.

Can I request extra cleaning tasks?

Yes, mention any extra tasks or areas needing special attention in your enquiry. We’ll confirm what can be included before you book.
