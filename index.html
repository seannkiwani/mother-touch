Questions

A few useful
answers.

Which cleaning services do you offer?

Residential, office, Airbnb and holiday-home cleaning, deep cleaning, and regular domestic cleaning. If what you need sits between two of those, just ask.

Do you supply your own cleaning products?

Yes. We bring our own products, cloths, mop and vacuum. If you’d prefer we use your products, say so when you book and we will.

Can I arrange regular cleaning?

Yes. Ask about weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits in your area. We hold the same slot each week so it’s predictable for you.

How do I request a quote?

Share your suburb, property size, the cleaning you need and a preferred date, by form or WhatsApp. We reply inside a working day.

Is an enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. The clean is confirmed with Mother Touch once the service, date and price are agreed.

How do you handle keys and access?

Whichever way suits you. Meet us there, leave a key with a neighbour or concierge, or use a key safe. For regular clients we can hold a key on file.
